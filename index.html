Projects

Temu Pricing Calculator

HTML · CSS · JavaScript

Built a pricing tool for selling gloves under Temu's fully-managed model. It calculates total cost (product, packaging, shipping, and loss from returns and damage), break-even price, and suggested price based on a target profit margin for 1, 6, 12, and 24-pair packs. When Temu offers a price, the tool instantly shows whether to accept it, the margin is thin, or it will lose money.

TikTok Shop · Temu

Product Assembly Manual: L-Shaped Electric Standing Desk

Adobe Illustrator · Internship

During my internship, I wrote the instructions and redesigned the layout of a 20-page assembly manual for an L-shaped electric standing desk sold in the Chinese market.
